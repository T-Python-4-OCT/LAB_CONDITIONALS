## Create a Python program that calculates the price of a movie ticket based on the customer's age, day of the week, and whether they have a student discount.

#### Ask the user for:

- Their age
- The day of the week
- Whether they are a student (yes or no)


#### Use these rules:

Base ticket price

- Under 5 years old → Free
- 5–12 years old → $6
- 13–59 years old → $10
- 60 years old or older → $7

Additional rules

- On Friday, everyone pays an extra $2.
- Students get 20% off, but only if the ticket is not free.
- If the user enters an invalid age (negative number), print:
"Invalid age"

- If the user enters a day that isn't one of:
Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday print: "Invalid day"

##### Example 1
```
Enter your age: 20
Enter the day: Friday
Are you a student? yes

Ticket price: $9.60
```

#### Example 2
```
Enter your age: 10
Enter the day: Monday
Are you a student? no

Ticket price: $6.00
```

#### Example 3
```
Enter your age: 4
Enter the day: Friday
Are you a student? yes

Ticket price: Free
```
